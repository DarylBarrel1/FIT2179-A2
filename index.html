Not all Australians have equal access to hospitals

Australia's healthcare system is world-class on average. But averages hide a troubling reality. Hospital resources are not evenly spread and those gaps quietly shape how long people live.

The map shows hospital bed density across Australia. The bar chart ranks states by life expectancy. The scatter plot reveals a surprising pattern and explains why the NT breaks all expectations.
